## 🔭 About OpenPMCA

OpenPMCA is an open-source initiative dedicated to maintaining, modernizing, and developing tools for Sony cameras powered by the PlayMemories Camera Apps subsystem. When official ecosystem support ends, community stewardship begins. We empower photographers, researchers, and developers to take full ownership of their camera hardware through open diagnostics, tweak utilities, and cross-platform tooling.

### 📷 For Photographers & Users
Get the most out of your camera in 3 simple steps:

1. **Download the App:** Grab the latest release of **[Sony-A7-PMCA](https://github.com/OpenPMCA/Sony-A7-PMCA/releases)** for macOS, Windows, or Linux.

## 📷 Camera Compatibility

OpenPMCA tools target Sony cameras featuring the PlayMemories Camera Apps platform (Gen 1 & Gen 2):

* **Sony Alpha Full Frame:** A7, A7 II, A7R, A7R II, A7S, A7S II
* **Sony Alpha APS-C:** A5000, A5100, A6000, A6300, A6500
* **Sony Cyber-shot & Compacts:** RX100 (II, III, IV, V), RX10 (I, II, III), RX1R II, HX series

*(Note: Newer cameras running the BIONZ XR OS or Android 9+ based firmware use different architectures and are not currently supported by PMCA tools).*
